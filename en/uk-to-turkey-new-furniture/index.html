New Furniture Shipping — UK to Turkey | Hanbros UK — Leader in UK–Turkey Logistics

Ordered furniture from a UK retailer or showroom and need it delivered to Turkey? Hanbros UK is the leading choice for new furniture shipping — direct from shop or warehouse to your Turkish home with full customs handling.

Many Turkish families and property investors buy sofas, kitchens and bedroom sets in the UK for their holiday homes or rental properties in Turkey. We collect from retailers (with your order reference), repack where needed and deliver with commercial invoices for smooth Turkish customs clearance.

Our New Furniture Services Include

  • Retailer collection: Pick-up from IKEA, DFS, local showrooms and online orders.
  • Flat-pack & assembled: Careful handling of boxed and built furniture.
  • Multi-item consignments: Combine several purchases into one cost-effective shipment.
  • Commercial invoices: Proper documentation for new goods import to Turkey.
  • Room-of-choice delivery: Delivered to your specified room in Turkey where access allows.

Why choose Hanbros UK? We are not a generic freight forwarder — we are built exclusively for the United Kingdom to Turkey corridor, with bilingual staff, transparent pricing and thousands of satisfied customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I pay import duty on new furniture?

New goods typically attract Turkish import VAT and duty. We provide transparent cost estimates before shipping.

Can you collect from any UK furniture store?

Yes — provide the retailer address, order number and collection date window.

How is new furniture protected in transit?

Export blankets, corner protectors and secure strapping in our dedicated furniture vehicles.
